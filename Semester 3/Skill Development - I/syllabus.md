# Skill Development - I

- Course code: SDV05601
- Course prerequisite: None

## Course Objectives

- To improve verbal and non-verbal communication skills of the students
- To enhance the ability of the students in reading and listening
- To develop skills of the students in writing different documents
- To increase awareness of emotions and SWOC

## Course Outcomes: CO609._

1. **Demonstrate** effective communication through email writing, presentations, and video introduction
2. **Construct** a professional resume, SOP, LinkedIn profile, and GitHub portfolio showcasing individual skills.
3. **Evaluate** personal strengths and areas of improvement using tools like SWOT and feedback from mock interviews
4. **Participate** in collaborative tasks and discussions to build teamwork, etiquette, and problem-solving abilities

## List of tutorial activities

1. Draft a professional email requesting leave from your project manager.
2. Create a 3-slide presentation (PPT) on "My Career Goals in IT".
3. Record a 2-minute video introducing yourself for a virtual job interview.
4. Create a professional resume in a given template (PDF and editable format).
5. Build a simple portfolio website or GitHub project profile page.
6. Create or update your LinkedIn profile and share the profile link.
7. Conduct a SWOT analysis on yourself and present the findings.
8. Prepare for and attend a mock interview conducted by peers and faculty.
9. Prepare a mind-map on "Qualities of a Good Team Member".
10. List 5 workplace scenarios and response using proper workplace etiquette.
11. Participate in a group discussion: "AI is replacing human jobs - agree or disagree?".
12. Role-play a mock team meeting with assigned roles (Team Lead, Developer, Tester).
